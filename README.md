## ✨ Features

- **📊 Comprehensive Dashboard:** Real-time summary cards tracking Total Tasks, Completed Tasks, Pending Tasks, and Overall Study Progress percentage.
- **📝 Full Task Management (CRUD):** 
  - Add, edit, and delete tasks seamlessly.
  - Interactive status toggling (Mark tasks as complete/incomplete).
  - Filter tasks dynamically by **Status** (Pending / Completed) and **Priority** (High / Medium / Low).
- **📅 Daily Study Planner & Calendar:** Interactive monthly calendar view where selecting any date displays and manages your hourly study sessions.
- **📈 Interactive Analytics:** Powered by **Chart.js** with visual breakdowns for Task Priority distribution and Weekly Study Hours.
- **🌗 Dark / Light Mode:** Smooth theme toggling that saves your preference automatically.
- **💾 LocalStorage Persistence:** All tasks, study sessions, and theme settings persist locally in your browser across refreshes.
